How it works

01

Snap it or describe it

Aholo Lux3D turns a photo, or a few words, into a 3D model of your car, scooter, door or bin.

02

Put it on your street

It stands at its real height on a photorealistic 3D map of your street, beside a person for scale.

03

Raise the water

Watch it reach the wheels, the windows, your waist. Check real river-flood data, then find higher ground.
